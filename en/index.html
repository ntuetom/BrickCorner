Learn it in 30 seconds

Three rules.
Endless mind games.

Opening

Start from your corner

Everyone's first piece must cover their own starting corner.

Corners ✓ Edges ✗

Same colour: corners only

Your new piece must touch your own pieces at a corner — never along an edge.

Block

Rivals can box you in

Different colours can touch freely. Take your rival's corners first and they run out of room.

Scoring: if you can't place a piece, you pass. At the end you lose a point for every square left in your hand, and placing everything earns a bonus — fewest squares left wins.

FAQ

Before you download

Is it free?

Free to download and play, with ads. For an ad-free game, subscribe to Premium (monthly or yearly), which also gives you a daily coin bonus plus free hints and undo. Prices are shown in the App Store for your region.

Do my friends need the app?

Yes — they'll need to download BrickCorner (it's free). Then they open Online Match, choose Join and enter your room code.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and play — no account and no personal details required.

How many people can play?

Up to 4 per room. AI can take any empty seats, and you can also play solo against the AI.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet — BrickCorner is available on iPhone and iPad (iOS 16 or later).

Need help?

Visit the support page to get in touch. We usually reply within 24–48 hours.
